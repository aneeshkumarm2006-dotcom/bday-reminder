Birthdays are scattered across your contacts, your group chats, and your memory. So you forget them — and you only find out when it's already too late to do anything about it.

Circle the date fixes that. Add someone once, and you'll get a reminder with enough time to actually do something: order the gift, book the table, or just send a message that arrives on the day instead of two days late.

WHAT YOU GET

• Reminders that arrive early
Choose how far ahead you want to know — a week, three days, the morning of. Reminders come as a push notification, in the app, or by email.

• Add people in seconds
Type a name and a date, import straight from your phone contacts, sync from Google Contacts, or paste in a spreadsheet. However your birthdays are stored today, they can come with you.

• A calendar you'll actually look at
See the whole month at a glance and know instantly who's coming up. Or open the list view for everything ahead in order.

• A home screen widget
Your next three birthdays, right on your home screen. No app to open, nothing to remember to check.

• Shared lists for family
Invite your family to a shared list and everyone sees the same birthdays. Add your niece's birthday once and your whole family gets reminded — no group chat required.

• Notes and gift ideas
Keep a note on anyone: their ring size, the book they mentioned, what you gave them last year. It's there waiting when the reminder arrives.

• Anniversaries and more
It's not only birthdays. Track anniversaries, memorials, and any date you don't want to lose.

FREE, WITH NO CATCH

Circle the date is free. There are no ads, no paid tier, no subscription, and no upsell waiting three screens in. We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.

The people you add are yours. We use that information to send your reminders and power your shared lists, and for nothing else. You can delete any person, note, or list whenever you like, and you can delete your whole account from Settings in one step.

BUILT TO BE QUIET

Most reminder apps want your attention every day. This one is designed to stay out of your way and speak up once, at the right moment, about someone you care about.

Add the people who matter. We'll handle the remembering.
